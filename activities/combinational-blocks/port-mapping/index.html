Port Mapping: Joining Blocks and Pinning Them to the Basys 3

1 Explore2 Predict3 Connect
Section 1 · Concept

One design, two blocks, one wire between them

A top-level design can contain no logic of its own. It only creates copies of blocks and joins them with a port map: each port => signal pairs a block’s port with a wire of the parent. Here parity3_gen makes a parity bit p, and word_driver shows the codeword d2 d1 d0 p on LEDs. The signal p is the only wire between them. A constraint file (.xdc) then ties the top-level ports to physical Basys 3 pins.

Objective. Trace every connection in the port map, then predict what the board LEDs will show.
